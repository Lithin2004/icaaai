SRKREC

Sagi Rama Krishnam Raju Engineering College (SRKR), established in 1980, is one of Andhra Pradesh's first self-financing engineering institutions, dedicated to empowering rural students through technical education. With its 30-acre green campus, the college offers state-of-the-art facilities that foster an inclusive and culturally responsive learning environment. Over the years, SRKR has built a strong ethos of creativity, confidence, and logical thinking in its students, preparing them for nation-building and diverse careers. The college's vision, inspired by its founder Sri Sagi Rama Krishnam Raju, continues under the leadership of Sri S. Prasad Raju, ensuring academic excellence. SRKR combines science, creativity, and critical thinking to address societal challenges, making it a hub for innovation and problem-solving. With 44 years of academic excellence, SRKR offers 12 B.Tech., 6 M.Tech., and 10 Ph.D. programs, alongside a B.B.A. program. Accredited with an ‘A+’ Grade by NAAC and multiple NBA accredited B.Tech. programs, the institution is recognized by AICTE and UGC for its outstanding contributions to education and research. SRKR’s initiatives include the AICTE IDEA Lab, Atal Community Innovation Centre, and Business Incubation Centre, emphasizing entrepreneurship, innovation, and skill development. Its 14 Centres of Excellence further drive research culture, solving societal challenges through research and development.

About Department CSE

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) at Sagi Rama Krishnam Raju Engineering College, established in 1991, has grown into a leading hub for teaching, research, and industry collaboration.The department offers B.Tech programmes in CSE, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Computer Science & Design, and CSE (IoT & Cyber Security with Blockchain), along with M.Tech in Computer Science & Technology and a Ph.D. programme. It also pioneers a Minor Degree in Quantum Technologies, aligned with India’s National Quantum Mission. With a team of highly qualified faculty, the department is recognized as a Research Centre under JNTU Kakinada and accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). Graduates are consistently placed in top multinational companies and product-based firms across domains such as software development, data science, cybersecurity, AI/ML, and cloud computing. The department has also hosted three international conferences and actively promotes innovation through hackathons, design sprints, and professional society activities.

ICAAAI Conference

The 3rd International Conference on Algorithms in Advanced Artificial Intelligence (ICAAAI-2025) provides a global platform for researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to exchange knowledge and showcase innovations in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and emerging computing technologies. Building on the success of its previous editions, the conference emphasizes both theoretical advances and real-world applications across interdisciplinary domains.

About Publication and Ethics Statement

Publication ethics for conferences are crucial for maintaining academic research integrity. Key principles include originality, plagiarism, authorship, conflicts of interest, fair peer review, avoiding publication bias, and avoiding redundant or duplicate publication. Authors must ensure their work is original, properly cite others' work, and disclose any conflicts of interest. Conference organizers should establish clear guidelines, ensure fair peer review, and prevent misconduct. Consequences of misconduct include retraction, notification, and sanctions, such as bans from submitting to future conferences or publications. Both parties must work together to ensure the integrity and credibility of academic research. Author Guidelines for Conference Papers

Double-Blind Peer Review: When submitting a conference paper, it's essential to adhere to specific guidelines to ensure your work is presented professionally and effectively. Here are some key guidelines to consider:

Paper Structure

  • Purpose/Objectives: Clearly state the purpose and objectives of your research.
  • Methodology/Approach: Describe your research methodology and approach.
  • Findings/Results: Present your findings and results.
  • Conclusion/Implications/Recommendations: Summarize your conclusions and implications, and provide recommendations.

Formatting Guidelines

  • Paper Length: 6–12 pages including references, tables/charts, and keywords.
  • Abstract Length: 200–250 words.
  • Keywords: 3–5 words.
  • Font: 12-point Times New Roman.
  • Paragraph: Single-spaced.
  • Margin: Top: 2.54 cm, Bottom: 2.54 cm; Left: 3.18 cm, Right: 3.18 cm.
  • Page Numbers: Bottom-centered.
  • Layout: One-column portrait or two-column format, depending on the conference requirements.

Submission Guidelines

  • File Format: PDF or MS Word-compatible.
  • Submission Method: Online submission or email, depending on the conference.
  • Originality: Ensure your work is original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere.

Peer Review Process

Most conferences use a double-blind peer review process, where reviewers evaluate manuscripts based on scientific criteria, novelty, and academic excellence.

Evaluation Criteria: Reviewers consider relevance, structure, research method, ethical conduct, language, innovativeness, references, and hypothesis.

Additional Tips

  • Proofread and Edit: Eliminate grammatical errors and improve readability.
  • Meet Deadlines: Submit on time and follow all deadlines.
  • Follow Ethical Standards: Cite sources, avoid plagiarism, and ensure originality.
  • Additional Instructions of paper: Author Guidelines and Instructions
  • Conference Paper Template: Link for template

About Guidelines

Manuscripts must be prepared using the official Conference Paper Template. Papers should not exceed 6 pages; up to 2 additional pages may be included with a fee of INR 1000 per page. Submissions must be original and not under review elsewhere.The similarity index must be below 12%, and manuscripts containing machine-generated content will not be considered. At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present at the conference. A duly signed Copyright Form is mandatory for publication. Certificates of presentation will be issued to all registered paper presenters.

Paper Submission

Papers must be submitted electronically in PDF format via the submission link provided on the conference website/brochure. All manuscripts will undergo a double-blind peer-review process.
